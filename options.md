# Options lane rules (paper, ledger equity $1,000)
Instruments: LONG calls and LONG puts only (single leg, defined risk = premium paid). No short options, no naked anything, no spreads until legging two orders is verified. No multi-leg.
Underlying: US-listed, price > $10, avg vol > 1M, optionable. Idea source = full-market stock scan (Research playbook sec 2) plus SPY/QQQ regime. Bullish setup -> call. Bearish setup (below falling SMA50 and SMA200, breakdown on volume) -> put. Neutral -> nothing.
Contract choice (Quant): expiry 14-45 days out (weekly or monthly, standard only); delta 0.40-0.60 (near the money); open interest >= 500 at that strike; bid-ask spread <= 10% of mid; use quote_option_chain, quote_stock_quote for the option code, quote_option_volatility for IV. Reject if IV rank is extreme and rising into an event (IV crush risk).
Sizing: premium paid per trade <= $120 (12% of ledger). One contract = premium x 100. Total open premium <= $400 (40%). Max 3 open positions, max 1 per underlying, max 2 per sector. Whole contracts, at least 1. If 1 contract costs more than $120, skip it.
Orders: LIMIT orders only, at mid or slightly above (never market). Code format for sim orders: no "US." prefix, e.g. AAPL261120C100000. qty = number of contracts. Side enum BUY=1, SELL=2 unverified: FIRST order must be checked via sim_trade_history_order_list and fixed or cancelled if wrong. Verify fills and record real fill price.
Events: no holding through the underlying's earnings (sell before), and no entry within 3 trading days before one. FOMC Oct 28 and GDP/PCE Oct 29: allowed only if expiry is after them AND Risk accepts the gap risk in writing; default = avoid new entries Oct 27-29.
Exits (Coordinator enforces at each 30-min check, limit sell at bid/mid, market only if urgent): stop = premium down 50% from entry; take profit 1: premium +60% sell half; then trail the rest at -30% from the highest premium seen; time exit at 5 days to expiry; exit if the underlying closes through the setup's invalidation level; never average down; never roll a loser.
Account guard: day loss >= 5% ($50) or week loss >= 10% ($100) = no new entries until next session/Monday. Equity <= $900: half size. <= $800: halt, owner decides. <= $750: close all, report to owner.
Gates (unchanged): Research PASS, Sentiment not red, Quant confirmed (specifies underlying level, contract, entry limit, exit levels, planned hold), Risk YES (recomputes premium, caps, events, regime, IV), Challenger NO VETO (second source for price, IV, earnings date). Doubt = NO. "No trade" is a valid result.
Ledger: ledger.csv columns are reused; symbol = option code, qty = contracts, entry/stop/t1 = premium per share, risk_usd = premium x 100 x qty. Add a note per trade in theses/.

## Fees (owner screenshot 2026-10-21, moomoo AU Fixed Plan)
Commission USD 0.10 + platform fee USD 0.40 = USD 0.50 per contract per side, before regulatory/clearing fees (assume ~USD 0.05-0.10 more per contract). Budget USD 1.20 per contract round trip (buy + sell). Paper account may not charge it; the ledger charges it anyway so results match real life.
Rule: Risk rejects a trade if round-trip fees > 5% of the take-profit-1 gain (a $0.30 option, +60% = $18 gain per contract, fee $1.20 = 6.7% -> reject). Prefer contracts with premium >= $1.00. Record fees_usd in ledger.csv on every fill.

## Entry timing filter (owner idea 2026-10-21: VWAP + EMA9) - FILTER ONLY, NOT A STRATEGY
Use only AFTER the daily setup, gates and contract choice are done, at the 30-min check. Use quote_history_kline 5-min bars (ktype 6, extended_time 0): VWAP = cumulative turnover / cumulative volume since the 9:30 ET open; EMA9 of 5-min closes.
CALL entry allowed only if price > VWAP and EMA9 > VWAP and EMA9 rising. PUT entry only if price < VWAP and EMA9 < VWAP and EMA9 falling. Otherwise wait for the next check (do not chase; the order expires at 15:50 ET if still not met).
Quick test 2026-10-21 (4 liquid names, 5 days, 5-min bars, 30-min forward return of the underlying): 28 crossover signals, mean +4 bps (0.04%), win rate 54%, vs baseline -3 bps / 45%. Sample far too small to prove an edge, and 4 bps does not cover ~USD 1.20 per contract fees. Keep as a veto filter only; re-test monthly with more data.

## Crypto options (owner order 2026-10-21 20:05)
Moomoo ONLY (owner order 2026-10-21: no Coinbase). Moomoo crypto_account_* is LIVE money (forbidden). Crypto options = US-listed options on crypto-linked tickers, traded in the moomoo PAPER account during US hours: IBIT, ETHA (spot ETFs), BITO, MSTR, COIN, MARA, RIOT, CLSK, HOOD (verify each has liquid chains: OI >= 500, spread <= 10%).
Same rules as all options. Extra: all crypto-linked names count as ONE sector bucket and one correlated position (max 1 open crypto option at a time, premium <= $120). Judge direction from the moomoo charts of IBIT/ETHA and the crypto stocks (quote_history_kline, quote_stock_quote). Do not call any Coinbase tool. Crypto trades 24/7 but these options only trade 9:30-16:00 ET, so weekend/overnight gaps hit the premium at the open: no entry on Fridays for expiry under 10 days; exit before the weekend if the premium is up 60% or more.
Research adds: ETF flow news, MSTR mNAV/premium, COIN and miner earnings dates (blackout as for any stock). Sentiment adds: crypto news (regulation, exchange hacks, ETF approvals).
