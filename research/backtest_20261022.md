# Backtest: skills strategies vs buy-and-hold (2026-10-22)
Data: moomoo daily bars, ~370 bars (~18 months) each, 16 liquid optionable names (SPY QQQ AAPL MSFT NVDA AMZN META GOOGL TSLA AMD NFLX AVGO JPM XOM COIN MSTR). Long only. Entry next-day open. Stop 1.5xATR(14) (2x for TrendRide), target 3R (10R/none for TrendRide), time stop, 0.1% round-trip cost. R = profit in units of initial risk. Harness: research/backtest_harness.py (reproducible).

## Findings (honest)
1. BUY-AND-HOLD WON OUTRIGHT. +327% for the basket over 18 months. No timing strategy came close. This window is a strong tech bull market, which flatters holding and punishes stops.
2. Best ACTIVE rule = trend-following that lets winners run (TrendRide): enter a 20-day breakout only when SMA20>SMA50>SMA200 (full up-stack), exit only on a close below SMA20. +0.23R/trade, PF 1.49, shallow -6.1R drawdown. Confirms: the edge was in RIDING trends, not frequent timing.
3. Fixed-target strategies (3R) got chopped: cutting winners at 3R while the trend ran for far more was the main cost. The 55-day breakout + volume filter was the WORST here (-0.82R, 18 trades) - too selective, entered late, stopped out.
4. Mean-reversion (buy RSI<35 in an uptrend) had the best per-trade stats (PF 2.30) but only 12 trades - unproven, do NOT deploy on this alone.
5. NOT backtestable here: the chart patterns (triangles, H&S, wedges, flags - visual screener), and the FMP-dependent skills (CANSLIM, VCP, PEAD, fundamentals). No fake numbers produced for these.

## Caveats (important)
- ONE 18-month sample, ONE regime (bull). In a sideways/bear market the ranking likely flips: stops protect, buy-hold suffers. Not out-of-sample tested.
- 16 names, tech-heavy = correlated. Not a broad universe.
- Stock backtest. Options add spread + theta + IV; a stock edge does not transfer 1:1. Short-dated options would have bled theta during the slow grind up; the trend-ride edge favors LONGER-dated calls held through the trend.
- Survivorship: these are today's leaders. Names that blew up are not in the basket.

## Implication for the options council
- Favor LONGER-dated calls (45+ DTE) on trend leaders, HELD while the trend holds (exit on SMA20/50 break), over frequent short-dated entries. Aligns with the multi-timeframe trend rule already adopted; reinforces "let winners run" over the fixed 3R target for the runner portion.
- Keep stops (the bull regime hid their value; they matter when the trend breaks).
- Do NOT adopt mean-reversion or pattern signals as live triggers on this evidence. Keep logging for a bigger test.
